Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Overview

Video Generator is a personal, single-user automation tool. This privacy policy explains how the tool handles data when interacting with third-party platforms such as TikTok.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

This tool does not collect, process, or store any personal data from third-party platform users. It does not have end users beyond its single operator.

3. Data Used by the Tool

The tool uses OAuth 2.0 access tokens issued by third-party platforms (e.g., TikTok) to authenticate and publish content on behalf of the operator's own accounts. These tokens are stored locally on the operator's personal device and are never shared with third parties.

4. Content Published

All content published through this tool is original content created and owned by the operator. No user-generated content from third parties is collected or republished.

5. Third-Party Platforms

When this tool interacts with platforms such as TikTok, those platforms' own privacy policies apply. This tool operates strictly within the permissions granted by the operator through the standard OAuth 2.0 authorization flow.
