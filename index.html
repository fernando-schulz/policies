schulz-video-generator

An automation tool for generating and publishing short-form video content across multiple social media platforms.

What It Does

🎬

Automated Video Generation

Generates short-form videos from scripts, combining AI voiceover, images, and background music automatically.

📅

Multi-Platform Scheduling

Schedules and publishes content to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels on a configurable posting calendar.

🔒

Secure OAuth Integration

Connects to platforms via official OAuth 2.0 flows. Tokens are stored locally and never shared with third parties.

⚡

End-to-End Pipeline

From script to published video: text-to-speech, subtitle generation, thumbnail creation, and upload — fully automated.

Platform Integrations

Publishes content through official APIs to the following platforms:

TikTok YouTube Shorts Instagram Reels
